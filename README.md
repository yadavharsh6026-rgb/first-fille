# Himshikhar Escapes — Himachal & Uttarakhand Tours & Travels

An interactive, responsive travel business website built with **React**, **Vite.js**, and **Tailwind CSS**, designed specifically for travelers exploring **Himachal Pradesh** and **Uttarakhand**.

Inspired by premium travel brand aesthetics featuring deep forest pine greens, golden-sunshine accents, curved travel trajectories, and floating polaroid destination cards.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Hero Section (Reference Design)
- **Signature Typography**: *"Your Next **Unforgettable** Journey Starts Here"* with warm cursive accent styling.
- **Flight & Road Trajectory**: Dotted SVG travel path with floating vehicle badges (Airplane, Mountain SUV/Tempo, River Raft, Compass).
- **Interactive Polaroid Photo Cards**: Tilted cards with drop shadows, coral location pin badges (Manali, Spiti Valley, Rishikesh, Kedarnath), and hover effects that filter or jump to packages.
- **Quick Search & Filter Bar**: Instant filtering by Destination Region, Travel Style, Duration, and Budget.

### 2. Handcrafted Tour Packages Catalog
- **8 Comprehensive Sample Packages**:
  - *Manali, Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel Snow Escapade* (5D/4N)
  - *Spiti Valley & Chandratal Lake Complete Circuit* (8D/7N)
  - *Kedarnath Dham & Chopta Tungnath Sacred Pilgrimage* (6D/5N)
  - *Rishikesh Whitewater Adventure & Auli Ski Paradise* (6D/5N)
  - *Romantic Shimla, Kullu & Manali Honeymoon Special* (6D/5N)
  - *Nainital, Mukteshwar & Jim Corbett Wildlife Safari* (5D/4N)
  - *Dharamshala, McLeodganj & Dalhousie Mini Switzerland* (6D/5N)
  - *Grand Chardham Yatra by Road & Helicopter* (10D/9N)
- Category tabs (All, Himachal, Uttarakhand, Chardham/Spiritual, Adventure & Treks, Honeymoon).
- Price and duration sorting with live search.

### 3. Interactive Multi-Tab Itinerary Modal
- Day-by-day accordion breakdown with meal plans and hotel stays.
- Itemized Inclusions (Hotels, Breakfast/Dinner, Private Cab, Permits) and Exclusions.
- Next available departure batches and custom departure date support.

### 4. Real-Time Booking & Reservation Engine
- **Dynamic Price Calculator**: Live recalculation based on Adults, Children, Room sharing, and Vehicle upgrades (Swift Dzire vs Innova Crysta vs Tempo Traveller).
- **Flexible Payment Modes**: 25% Advance Token Deposit or 100% Full Payment.
- **Booking Voucher Generation**: Unique Booking Reference Code (e.g., `HIM-2026-7842`), itemized voucher breakdown, and celebratory confetti animation (`canvas-confetti`).
- **Direct WhatsApp Concierge**: Instant 1-click sharing of the booking voucher to WhatsApp.

### 5. Custom Himalayan Trip Planner Wizard
- Step-by-step interactive builder to customize destinations, travel style, hotel tiers (3★ to 5★ luxury cottages), duration, and group size with instant cost estimation.

### 6. Destination Explorer & Chardham Spotlight
- Interactive region switcher for Himachal Pradesh vs Uttarakhand.
- Dedicated Garhwal Chardham Yatra & Kedarnath Helicopter shuttle desk.

### 7. Trust & Conversion Drivers
- Local Himachali advantage & vetted mountain drivers.
- Verified Google Reviews & traveler testimonials.
- Essential mountain travel FAQs (permits, AMS prevention, winter packing).
- Floating WhatsApp chat & instant call helpline.
